What is WhisperLedger?

WhisperLedger is a review-first daily expense tracker and household financial ledger designed for India. It captures everyday INR spending, simplifies shared household splits, and follows up on refunds and money owed—without ads, intrusive bank logins, or spreadsheets.

Who Created WhisperLedger?

WhisperLedger was conceived, designed, and created by Tanmay Agarwal as an offline-ready, privacy-respecting financial ledger built specifically for Indian expense and household patterns.

What is the Prime Goal of WhisperLedger?

The primary goal of WhisperLedger is to help you maintain a clean, frictionless record of where your money moves, eliminate group-chat math for shared flat expenses, and ensure forgotten refunds are recovered—all while keeping your financial data completely private and on-device.
