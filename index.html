What is WhisperLedger?

WhisperLedger is a review-first daily expense tracker and household financial ledger designed for India. It captures everyday INR spending, simplifies shared household splits, and follows up on refunds and money owed—without ads, intrusive bank logins, or spreadsheets.

Who Created WhisperLedger?

WhisperLedger is designed around everyday INR spending, household cost sharing, and money due back.

What is the Prime Goal of WhisperLedger?

It helps you keep the context around personal spending, shared balances, and follow-up items clear and actionable.
